Customers & Suppliers pages — pick one

Three designs for the two pages (they share one layout). Each is live: switch between Customers and Suppliers, search, use ↑ ↓ to walk the list, click the buttons. The names and figures are made up.

1Red cardThe list shows whole names (two lines when long) with the debt-age dot. The one picked on a red card: outstanding, limit, last payment, and a bar of how old the debt is. The six main buttons in one dark row, the rest under More.
2TabsLight and calm: Customers or Suppliers picked at the top of the list, filters as two drop-downs. A clean header with four figures, every button as an icon, and Overview · Unpaid bills · Payments · Notes as tabs.
3Table + panelEveryone in one wide table — whole name, mobile, outstanding, oldest bill, limit used, last payment — the boxes on top as filters. Click a row (or Enter) and their details slide in on the right with every button.
