Customers & Suppliers pages — pick one

Six designs for the two pages (4, 5 and 6 are new) (they share one layout). Each is live: switch between Customers and Suppliers, search, use ↑ ↓ to walk the list, click the buttons. The names and figures are made up.

1Red cardThe list shows whole names (two lines when long) with the debt-age dot. The one picked on a red card: outstanding, limit, last payment, and a bar of how old the debt is. The six main buttons in one dark row, the rest under More.
2TabsLight and calm: Customers or Suppliers picked at the top of the list, filters as two drop-downs. A clean header with four figures, every button as an icon, and Overview · Unpaid bills · Payments · Notes as tabs.
3Table + panelEveryone in one wide table — whole name, mobile, outstanding, oldest bill, limit used, last payment — the boxes on top as filters. Click a row (or Enter) and their details slide in on the right with every button.
4StatementThree columns. A plain list on the left, the account in the middle like a printed statement — bills and payments with a running balance down to "Outstanding now" — and every button down the right side, grouped Money · Talk to them · Other.
5TilesA dark list with a debt-age bar under each name. On the right: all buttons in one row with pictures, four figure tiles, and the unpaid bills with tick boxes — tick the ones being paid and press Take payment.
6Cards + drawerEveryone as a card across the whole screen — colour tells how old the debt is, a bar shows the limit used. Click a card (or search and Enter) and a red panel slides in from the right with the figures, all buttons and the bills. Esc closes it.
