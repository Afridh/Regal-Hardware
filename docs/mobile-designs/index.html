Mobile billing — pick one

Three easy ways to make a bill on a phone. Each one works: search, add items, change the quantity, pick the customer and take the money. The products, prices and customers are made up.

1Tap the tilesThe items you sell most are big tiles — one tap adds one. A dark bar at the bottom always shows the total; Pay opens the money sheet.
2Scan and goThe camera is ready at the top — point at the barcode and it beeps on. No barcode? Type a name. The bill fills underneath, the total and Pay stay at the foot.
3One step at a timeThree plain steps with one big button each: what are they buying → who is it for → how are they paying. Hard to get wrong for anybody new.
