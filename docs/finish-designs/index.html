The end of a bill — pick one

Three designs for the payment window, "Who sold it?" and the choice of bill to print. Each is live and goes through all three: type an amount and press Enter (cash), A (card), C (credit)…, then P to print; pick who sold it with the arrows and Enter; then 1–4 for the bill. The bar at the top jumps between the three. The bill and the staff are made up.

1Red cardLike your new Ctrl+M and Put on account: the net total and the amount on a red card, what has been taken in the middle with a bar, the ways to pay as a calm list with their keys, a big PRINT. Salespeople first in "Who sold it?"; the bills as four big tiles.
2CounterDark, like a till: the amount on a big green display, each way to pay a coloured key (Cash the biggest), and what is taken printed on a paper slip with the change. Staff as cards; each bill shown as a little picture of the paper.
3Side sheetLight and simple, from the right like Put on account, so the bill stays in sight: the amount, the ways to pay as buttons, a coloured bar of what is paid how, and the change and PRINT at the foot.
