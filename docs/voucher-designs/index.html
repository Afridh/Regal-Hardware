Receipt voucher — pick one

Three ways to draw the voucher a customer gets when they pay. In all three the customer's name sits where it does on the invoice (the box on the right), in plain letters, and the voucher shows what they owed before, what they paid, and what is left. A5 landscape, the same as the bill. The names and figures are made up.

1Like the invoiceLaid out exactly as the A5 bill. How it was paid in the table; the account in the box at the bottom right — previous balance, paid now, balance.
2Before − paid = nowThe three figures large across the middle, so the customer sees at a glance what came off. How it was paid underneath.
3Short statementReads like a little statement: brought forward, less each payment, balance now. Beside it, the bills the money went to and what is still open on each.
