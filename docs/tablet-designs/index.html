Tablet product view — pick one

A special page for the tablet, made for someone who does not want to search by product names. Every product shows as a big picture with a big price and whether it is in stock. Tap a picture to see it large. You can also type a few letters, or press the red 🎤 and say it. The A / A+ / A++ buttons make everything bigger. These use pictures drawn in for now — the real page would show each product's own photo. The products and prices are made up.

1Picture shelvesOpens on big picture tiles for each kind of goods, in English and Sinhala. Tap one and its products come up as large pictures with the price. A big Back button always takes you home.
2Like the shop wallThe kinds of goods stay down the left like the shop's shelves; tap one and the pictures fill the right side. Nothing to go back from — just tap another shelf.
3Ask and seeOne big question in the middle: "What do you need?" Type it or say it. Below, the things sold most are already showing, as big rows with the picture, price, stock and where it is kept.
