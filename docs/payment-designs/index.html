Customer payment (Ctrl+M) — pick one

Three designs for the window that finds who is paying. Each is live: open it, type a name, a mobile or the first letters (try "gv" or "silva"), press the arrows and Enter. The customers are made up.

1Clear listYour window, made easier to read: a coloured badge for how old the debt is, bills open, how near their limit, and filters (Tab).
2Look before you takeThe list on the left; on the right the customer in red — the outstanding, their open bills oldest first, the last payment. Enter pays, F2 picks bills.
3Quick pickA dark search bar. Before you type: the six who owe the most as tiles (keys 1–6) and who paid lately. Type and it becomes a list.
