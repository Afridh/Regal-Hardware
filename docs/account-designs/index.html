Put on account (side sheet) — pick one

Three designs for the sheet that puts a bill on a customer's account. Each is live: type a name, use the arrows and Enter to pick, then type what is paid now and press Enter to save (Esc goes back). Pick someone near their limit to see the "Allow it" step. The customers are made up.

1Red cardMatches the new Ctrl+M window: coloured dots for how old the debt is, then a red card — outstanding now → after this bill, with the limit bar showing what this bill adds.
2The sumThe bill total big at the top; customers as cards; then the sum written out like on paper: outstanding + this bill − paid now = outstanding after.
3Steps1 Who · 2 Paid now · 3 The bill, ticked off as you go, in the till's blue; a dark bar at the foot always shows what goes on account, the outstanding after, and Save.
